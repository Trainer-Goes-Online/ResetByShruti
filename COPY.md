## §16 · The Overlap Method · `[Schwartz: new mechanism]` · `[3/3]` ← the page's one heavy moment

> ### How **The Overlap Method** Works Differently
> Most plans pick one of your three problems. This one sequences all three, in an order that matters.

**01 · Gut first, not deficit first.**
Week one isn't a diet. It's flushing water retention and calming digestion — seed cycling for PCOS, selenium and thyroid-supportive foods on top. You feel lighter in three to four days: puffiness down, rings and shoes looser. Most of that is water, and Shruti will tell you so.

**02 · A deficit small enough to survive your week.**
Week two adds a slight deficit and one lighter detox day. The scale moves — often a kilo or two, partly water. Fluctuation is expected, and you're told that up front instead of being left to panic about it.

**03 · More food, not less.**
Week three raises protein and fiber. You eat *more*, the deficit gets easier to hold, and satiety does the work discipline was doing. Skin calms, digestion settles, jeans loosen — usually an inch to an inch and a half.

**04 · Reintroduce, then hold.**
Week four brings foods back on a steady five-meal pattern with a lighter dinner. Rice, roti and gluten were never removed permanently — they were limited and returned. That's the difference between a result you keep and one you escape from.

> **The layer underneath all four:** your PCOS protocol, your thyroid protocol and your insulin-resistance protocol run *simultaneously* inside those weeks. That's the overlap. That's the part nobody else is doing.

> **6 Questions From Women Like You** — *Straight Answers. No Sales Spin.*

**Q.01 — Is this just another PCOS diet?** *(pre-opened, the hero objection)*
No, and the difference is what it treats. A PCOS plan works on your PCOS. Reset sequences your PCOS, your thyroid and your insulin resistance together, because in most of the women who come to us all three are running at once. Treating one and leaving two is why the last plan stalled.

**Q.02 — Is this a sales call?**
No. It's diagnostic. Shruti will tell you what's actually going on with your markers and what she'd do about it. If Reset is a fit, she'll walk you through it at the end. If it isn't, she'll say so and refund your ₹97. You will not be asked to commit to anything financially on the call.

**Q.03 — Will this work with a 12-hour desk job and no time to cook?**
That's the design input, not the exception. Every plan is built around the week you actually have — your hours, your commute, what's realistically cookable. One of the clients above is a corporate lawyer working 12–16 hour days.

**Q.04 — I've tried everything and nothing lasted. Why is this different?**
Because nothing you tried was wrong about the food. It was incomplete about the cause. When a plan addresses one of three overlapping problems, it works for a few weeks and then stops — which is exactly what you experienced, and exactly what you were told was a discipline problem.

**Q.05 — Am I locked into anything?**
No. The ₹97 books a call. Nothing else is decided until you decide it.

**Q.06 — How long until I actually see something?**
You'll feel lighter in the first three or four days — less bloating, less puffiness, rings and shoes looser. Most of that is water, and you should know that going in. Real fat loss shows around weeks three to four. Markers like TSH and HbA1c move over months, not weeks — Simran's TSH took the full twelve. Anyone promising faster is selling you something.
